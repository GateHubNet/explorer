Flags

/v2/mpt/{issuanceId}

Set when the issuance was created. They cannot change.

Can lockYesThe issuer can lock one holder’s balance, or all of HTF, so it cannot move.
Require authYesAn account needs the issuer’s approval before it can hold HTF.
Can escrowNoHTF cannot be held in escrows.
Can tradeYesHTF can be traded on the DEX.
Can transferYesHolders can send HTF to each other, not only back to the issuer.
Can clawbackYesThe issuer can take HTF back from a holder.
