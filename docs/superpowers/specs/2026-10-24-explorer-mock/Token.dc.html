Issuer settings

/v2/tokens/{currency}.{issuer}

Set on the issuer’s account. They apply to every holder of NPUSD.

Transfer feeNoneHolders send NPUSD to each other at no extra cost.
Global freezeOffNPUSD moves freely. The issuer has not frozen it for everyone.
No freezeNot setThe issuer has kept the right to freeze: it can still freeze one holder’s trust line, or all of NPUSD.
ClawbackAllowedThe issuer can take NPUSD back from a holder’s trust line.
ClawbackNot allowedThe issuer cannot take NPUSD back from a holder.
Require authOffAnyone can open a trust line and hold NPUSD, without the issuer’s approval.
Default rippleOnHolders can pay each other in NPUSD through the issuer’s account.
